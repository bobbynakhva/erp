07 / Questions, answered

Manufacturing ERP.
The practical questions.

What is Tamba ERP?

Tamba ERP is manufacturing ERP software that connects production, planning, inventory, purchasing, sales, subcontracting, dispatch and GST reports. It gives factory owners and teams one place to manage connected operational work.

Is Tamba ERP suitable for brass parts manufacturers?

Tamba ERP supports core workflows relevant to brass parts manufacturing: material movements, work orders, purchasing, subcontracting, inspection and delivery challans. It was built with input from working factories in Jamnagar. Review your specific production processes with the product team before choosing a setup.

Does Tamba ERP include inventory and GST reporting?

Yes. Tamba ERP includes inventory and material movements, purchasing, sales and GST reports. Reporting features support business workflows; they do not replace professional accounting or tax advice.

Can manufacturers outside Jamnagar use Tamba ERP?

Tamba ERP is intended for manufacturers beyond Jamnagar as well as local brass factories. The product team should assess each factory's workflow and setup requirements rather than assuming every industry uses the same process.

How do employees access their company's ERP?

Each manufacturer uses a separate ERP deployment and database at its own company domain. A manufacturer administrator completes first-time company setup. Employees sign in through that company's ERP address, not the public Tamba website or the private product-owner portal.
